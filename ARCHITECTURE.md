# SozoRock Health Agentic architecture

SozoRock Health Agentic supports non-clinical, evidence-based planning. Evidence sources, gaps, uncertainty, and human judgment remain visible in planning and decision-making.

The CB-CAP institutional runtime is inactive. Activation requires reviewed readiness, security, recovery, and human-review checks. Public evidence services are separate from the institutional runtime.

Private evidence must remain access-controlled and tenant-separated. Human review is required before actions are taken.
